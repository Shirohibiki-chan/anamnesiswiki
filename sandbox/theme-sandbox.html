Fonts

✦ Fraunces, Inter, Newsreader already ship with the app. Everything in the big groups is a free font sitting right here in the sandbox — pick any of them and I can bundle it for real, it's a five-minute job. The last group, marked ·, is fonts Windows gave you; those can't legally be shipped, so they'd turn into something else on anyone else's computer.
