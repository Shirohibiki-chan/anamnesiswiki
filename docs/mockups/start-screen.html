Manage pins

Behind "Manage pins"

Reordering by dragging a card along a row is miserable, so the row doesn't do it. This does: full-width rows with a grip and a position, and every world you haven't pinned right underneath, so pinning and rearranging are the same trip. The dot on a cover means it's already up there.

What separates them

  • Hearth is fastest to get back to writing, and best if you mostly live in one world. It gets emptier-looking the more worlds you have.
  • Atlas looks the best in a screenshot and scales to many worlds, but leans on cover pictures — that's a real feature, not just styling.
  • Codex needs no new features and holds up at any number of worlds. It's the most distinctive and the least like anything you showed me.

All of them keep the second-window behaviour in mind: a world already open elsewhere would show as marked and unclickable, rather than letting you open it twice.
